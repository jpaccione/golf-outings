Event Details & Inclusions

🏌️ Outing Package Details ($130 All-In)

  • • Full 18-hole green fee + golf cart
  • • Practice range balls prior to tee time
  • • Sleeve of golf balls
  • • Course capacity locked at 6 tee times (24 spots)
  • • Optional Skins Game: $10 cash buy-in. Birdie or better wins a skin; ties carry over.

🍔 Included Lunch & Drink Tickets

  • • Lunch Ticket: Choice of Hamburger, Hot Dog, or Bratwurst + Bag of Chips + Water/Soft Drink (Valid anytime before, during, or after round)
  • • Drink Ticket: Choice of Water, Soft Drink, or Beer
  • • Non-Playing Rider: Cart seat + Drink & Lunch tickets

Day-of Logistics & Pro-Tips

⏰ Arrival & Check-In

Please arrive at least 30–45 minutes prior to your assigned tee time. Head directly to the Pro Shop inside the clubhouse to check in, collect your range ball token, sleeve of golf balls, and meal/drink vouchers.

⛳ Practice Facilities & Range

Full-size driving range and putting green are located right outside the clubhouse. Range balls are included in your package—simply ask the Pro Shop attendant for your bucket.

🏆 Optional Skins Game ($10 Cash)

We will have an optional $10 cash buy-in skins pot ($10 cash due at morning check-in). Format: Birdie or better wins a skin. Ties carry over, adding another skin to the next win!

👕 Course Dress Code

Standard golf attire is required (collared shirts, no denim or cargo shorts, and soft spikes/sneakers only).
